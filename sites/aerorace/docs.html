TECHNICAL REGULATIONS (2026)

To maintain parity and pilot safety, all aircraft must pass pre-race scruitineering at the official Paddock Weigh-In Station.

1. QUADCOPTER SPECIFICATIONS

  • Weight Max Take-Off Weight (AUW): 650 grams (incl. 6S LiPo)
  • Props Maximum Size: 5.1 inches (3-blade only)
  • Frame Wheelbase: 210mm - 235mm true-X carbon fiber
  • Battery Limit: 6S LiHV (26.1V max initial charge)
