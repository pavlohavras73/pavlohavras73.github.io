Phone Forwarding Guide

Setting up VoxDesk is incredibly simple. All you need to do is forward your existing business number to your dedicated VoxDesk AI number.

Step 1: Retrieve your VoxDesk Number

Log into your dashboard and navigate to the Settings tab. You will see your assigned local or toll-free AI number.

Step 2: Configure Call Forwarding

Depending on your carrier, the steps vary slightly:

  • Verizon/AT&T: Dial *72 followed by your VoxDesk number.
  • RingCentral/Zoom Phone: Navigate to your call handling rules and set "Forward to External Number".
  • Google Voice: Go to settings, linked numbers, and add your VoxDesk number.

Step 3: Test the Connection

Call your primary business number from a personal phone. You should be greeted by your AI receptionist within 2 rings.
