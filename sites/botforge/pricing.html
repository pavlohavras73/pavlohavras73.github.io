Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need my own server or VPS?

No. BotForge runs fully managed on global edge infrastructure. You only need your Telegram BotFather token.

How does Telegram Stars payout work?

BotForge integrates directly with Telegram's native invoice system. Stars accumulate in your Fragment/Telegram wallet.

Can I export my bot code or webhooks?

Yes! You can export your bot logic as structured JSON or forward events to custom REST endpoints anytime.

What happens if I exceed message limits?

We never shut off your bot abruptly. Extra volume is billed at a transparent $0.15 per 1,000 messages.
